# Contributor License Agreement

> **⚠️ This document has not been reviewed by legal counsel.** It is drafted on the
> structure of the Apache Individual Contributor License Agreement v2.0, with an
> added relicensing grant needed for dual licensing. Have a lawyer review it before
> you require anyone to sign it. Replace every `[BRACKETED]` value.

## Why this exists

Orangescrum Community Edition is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public
License v3.0. Andolasoft Inc. also distributes a commercially licensed edition that
shares code with this project.

Because of that, Andolasoft Inc. needs permission from every contributor to use
their contribution in **both** editions. Without it, a contribution accepted here
under AGPL v3 could not be included in the commercial edition. This agreement
grants that permission. You keep the copyright to your work.

If you are contributing on behalf of an employer, you likely need the Corporate CLA
instead — see [Corporate contributions](#corporate-contributions).

## How to sign

Add a line to `CONTRIBUTORS.md` in your first pull request:

```
Full Name <email@example.com> — signed CLA YYYY-MM-DD
```

Opening a pull request that includes that line constitutes your acceptance of the
terms above.

> Consider automating this instead. [CLA Assistant](https://github.com/cla-assistant/cla-assistant)
> is free for public repositories and records signatures against each PR, which is
> far more defensible than a hand-maintained file.

## Corporate contributions

If you are contributing as part of your employment, or your employer has rights to
the IP you create, your employer must sign a **Corporate CLA** before your
contributions can be accepted. A CCLA is a separate document that lists the
employees authorized to contribute. Contact [CONTACT EMAIL] to arrange one.

This repository does not yet include a CCLA template. The Apache Corporate CLA is a
reasonable starting point, adjusted to include the relicensing grant in section 4
above.
